# Question

What does the eight-session group authorization establish, and how does it compare with the number of distinct group sessions Rowan actually attended during the episode? Identify the attended encounter dates and explain why the authorization quantity cannot stand in for attendance.

# Answer

The authorization approved **up to eight scheduled group sessions** between January 5 and January 30, 2026. It established permission to arrange group appointments—not that eight groups were held or that Rowan attended them. The letter expressly says no attendance record accompanied it. [BH-D001:L10,L12,L15]

The episode records establish **five distinct attended group encounters**, three fewer than the authorized quantity:

| Attended date | Encounter | Attendance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| January 6 | HG-E102 | Roster records partial attendance; facilitator documents Rowan’s participation. [BH-D005:L9,L10] [BH-D004:L14] |
| January 12 | HG-E105 | Roster records full attendance; facilitator documents participation. [BH-D005:L9,L11] [BH-D009:L14,L16] |
| January 19 | HG-E110 | Clinical record documents participation; a signed correction establishes departure at 11:15 rather than the original roster’s 11:30. [BH-D101:L3,L4,L10] [BH-D103:L5,L7,L9,L15,L16] |
| January 22 | HG-E113 | Final register records attendance after a late arrival; activity record documents participation. [BH-D108:L8,L9,L14] [BH-D107:L7,L9] |
| January 29 | HG-E118 | Final register records attendance; activity record documents participation. [BH-D108:L8,L12,L20] [BH-D107:L12,L14] |

The count is **1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 5 distinct encounters**, not eight. Scheduled slots cannot fill the difference: the January 15 group was cancelled and no group was held, while the final January 27 roster records Rowan as a no-show with no treatment contact. [BH-D016:L9,L15] [BH-D108:L10,L16]
