# Question

The January 26 individual psychotherapy appointment has two signed clinician records. What count and patient-contact duration can be supported, why do the records differ, and what additional evidence would settle the duration?

# Answer

**Count one delivered individual psychotherapy encounter, not two.** Both final, signed records identify the same January 26 encounter and appointment; one is the primary clinician’s record and the other is the participating clinician’s record. [BH-D110:L3,L6,L15,L16] [BH-D111:L3,L4,L8,L16,L17]

**Patient-contact duration remains 40–50 minutes.** The primary record reports 09:00–09:50 (50 minutes). The participating clinician reports 09:10–09:50 (40 minutes) and says the patient entered the treatment room and the session began at 09:10. The records agree on the 09:50 end but conflict on the start; neither establishes that it corrects the other. [BH-D110:L7] [BH-D111:L7,L10,L14]

A contemporaneous patient-entry or contact-time record, or a signed clarification from the clinicians addressing **whether psychotherapy contact occurred from 09:00 to 09:10**, would settle which duration to use. The record provided does not establish an explanation for the discrepancy.
