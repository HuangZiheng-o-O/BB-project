# Question

Summarize the documented symptom course during the episode and the reason for the additional individual contact on January 19. Which symptom assessments are distinct, and what conclusions about progress can and cannot be supported?

# Answer

**Symptom course.** At the January 5 intake, Rowan reported several weeks of low mood, reduced interest, fragmented sleep, difficulty starting ordinary tasks, and worry and avoidance about returning to work; the PHQ-9 score was 18. [BH-D002:L11,L13,L15] By January 14, Rowan had opened a work message and taken short walks, but had not replied; sleep and morning initiation remained difficult. [BH-D011:L11,L15] On January 16, Rowan reported that getting out of the apartment was a little easier, while work-related avoidance and inconsistent sleep persisted. The clinician judged this *some improvement* in depressive symptoms, not an established routine or resolution of functional problems. [BH-D013:L11,L13]

**January 19 contact.** An additional, completed individual visit was added because Rowan became anxious during group when others discussed workplace demands. Rowan described feeling overwhelmed about keeping up on returning to work, with muscle tension, rapid breathing, and an urge to leave. Grounding and coping-card review eased the immediate anxiety enough to discuss drafting two sentences to a supervisor; sending them that day was not required. Avoidance and disrupted sleep still interfered with the usual work routine. [BH-D105:L6,L9-L13]

Subsequently, Rowan drafted but had not sent the message by January 21, and described variable sleep. By January 26, Rowan had sent it, but was postponing the next conversation and remained worried about what might be asked. [BH-D106:L9-L11] [BH-D110:L9-L13] The January 30 review described partial improvement, with continued sleep difficulty, avoidance, and meaningful work-related functional impact. [BH-D115:L8-L12]

**Distinct assessments and limits of the conclusion.** There are **three distinct PHQ-9 completions**: January 5 **18**, January 16 **14**, and January 30 **10**—a four-point decrease at each interval and eight points from first to last. [BH-D002:L15] [BH-D013:L6-L8] [BH-D115:L5-L6] The January 26 chart import is **not a fourth assessment**: it copies the January 16 score and form HG-Q116, with no new questionnaire. [BH-D014:L12-L17] These scores and the clinical notes support partial symptom improvement and some concrete steps toward work communication. They do **not** establish remission, a reliable routine, sustained return-to-work functioning, or that the improvement persisted beyond the documented episode; avoidance, anxiety when tasks expanded, and sleep disruption remained documented at the last review. [BH-D013:L13] [BH-D115:L8-L12]
