# Question

The January 22 clinical group note mentions Rowan's participation. Does it prove attendance for the entire scheduled group? Reconcile it with the patient attendance register.

# Answer

No. The clinical note establishes that Rowan participated, but it says Rowan joined after the discussion began and directs the reader to the separate attendance register for arrival and departure. The 10:00–11:30 time in the note is the **scheduled group**, not a claim that Rowan attended from 10:00. [BH-D107:L7,L8,L9,L17]

The attendance register records a **10:30 arrival**, an **11:30 departure**, and “Attended, late arrival”; its signed attestation says Rowan remained until the group closed. [BH-D108:L6,L8,L9,L14] Thus Rowan attended the final 60 minutes of the scheduled 90-minute span, not the entire group. Of those 60 minutes, the note identifies 10:45–11:00 as a nontherapeutic break, leaving **45 minutes of group treatment**: 15 minutes from 10:30–10:45 plus 30 minutes from 11:00–11:30. [BH-D107:L8,L17]
