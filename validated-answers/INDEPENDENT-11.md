# Question

Why did the January 15 skills group not occur, and is it accurate to characterize that date as Rowan failing to attend a held group?

# Answer

The January 15 coping skills group did not occur because the **clinic cancelled it due to staff illness**, and no covering facilitator was available. Rowan received the cancellation notice before the scheduled start and called to acknowledge it. The cancellation record says **no group was held, no participants were seen, and no replacement group took place in that time slot**. [BH-D016:L9,L11,L13,L15]

No. It would be inaccurate to describe January 15 as Rowan failing to attend a held group. The appointment was marked “Clinic cancelled,” not as a patient no-show. [BH-D006:L17,L20]
