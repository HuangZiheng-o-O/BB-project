# Question

Do January 30 recommendations to continue individual and group treatment establish that those services were actually delivered after January 30? What is the evidentiary limit?

# Answer

No. The January 30 clinician review **recommends continuity** of the established individual and group schedule; it does not document that either service was delivered later [BH-D115:L10,L12]. Rowan’s agreement to attend scheduled follow-up likewise records an intention, not attendance [BH-D114:L12].

The evidentiary limit is that the reviewed record covers services **through January 30**. Its attendance extract was prepared that day [BH-D108:L5,L8]. It cannot establish whether individual or group treatment occurred **after** January 30; that would require later attendance or treatment records.
